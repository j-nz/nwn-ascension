# ANPHILLIA-009 — Consensual sparring / duel damage mode

**Status:** RESEARCH FIRST  
**Parent:** ANPHILLIA-000  
**Donor:** https://github.com/mtijanic/anphillia/tree/master/factions

## Objective
Evaluate Anphillia's sparring concept as a safe consensual Ascension training/duel mode without faction-reputation logic.

## Required design
Audit current PvP, hostility, death/dying, resurrection and Training Hall rules. Require explicit mutual consent and reliable cancellation. Resolve lethal damage, on-hit effects, summons, AoE, traps, persistent damage and third-party interference. Sparring must not accidentally affect faction/crime/investigation/death penalties/PvP statistics/loot.

## Guardrail
Prefer designated training areas; no participant can force another PC into the mode.

## Acceptance
No implementation until edge cases are resolved; any prototype is area-gated and opt-in; disconnect/transition cleanup is reliable; normal PvP rules cannot be bypassed outside sparring.
