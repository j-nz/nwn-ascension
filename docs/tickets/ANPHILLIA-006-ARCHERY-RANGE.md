# ANPHILLIA-006 — Training Hall archery range scoring

**Status:** OPEN  
**Parent:** ANPHILLIA-000  
**Donor:** https://github.com/mtijanic/anphillia/blob/master/placeables/plc_archery_attk.nss

## Objective
Adapt Anphillia's scored archery target into a polished Training Hall activity.

## Requirements
Score supported ranged weapon attacks using attack quality and distance; provide concise score/bullseye feedback; respect relevant proficiency/focus/specialisation where appropriate; reuse Training Hall safety conventions; use stock NWN:EE assets; prevent ammunition/item generation and all XP/gold/progression farming.

## Acceptance
Supported bows/crossbows/sling are explicitly defined; melee/spells do not produce misleading scores; distance matters; no reward exploit exists; builder setup is documented.
